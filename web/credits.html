Credits

Lawn Wrangler 1.0.0 by Zeke (ElSpaniard97).

Art and sound

Every model and sound in the game is generated by its own code when the yard loads. The photo textures on the lawn, fence, houses and gardens come from a texture sheet supplied by the game's maker, Zeke; each is listed in ASSET_LICENSES.md. No outside sound or font files are used.
