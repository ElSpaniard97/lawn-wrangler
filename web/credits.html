Credits

Lawn Wrangler 1.0.0 by Zeke (ElSpaniard97).

Art and sound

Every sound in the game, and every model not listed below, is generated by its own code when the yard loads. The photo textures on the lawn, fence, houses and gardens come from a texture sheet supplied by the game's maker, Zeke; each is listed in ASSET_LICENSES.md. No outside sound or font files are used.

3D models

The browser edition uses these free models from Sketchfab, shrunk for the web (smaller textures, the walk animation only, the tractor's tyres split by axle and the three oaks split apart). Thank you to their authors.

  • "Lawn Tractor" by mirrol, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The riding mower in the browser edition.
  • "Bearded man - Low poly animated" by Agor_2012, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The landscaper, riding and walking in the browser edition.
  • "String trimmer" by Colin Charles, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The weed eater in the browser edition.
  • "Suburban House" by mbuannoart, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The house and porch in the browser edition.
  • "Oak Trees FREE Low Poly" by LordSamueliSolo, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The three shade trees in the yard in the browser edition.
